Data collection comes first. In Content Manager, choose the car, track/layout, tire and physical weather setting. Select WIMNA AI Thermal for automatic AI control; select WIMNA Test or Practice for manual collection. In the WIMNA app, enter a tire test label (X/Y/Z), requested compound, condition label and optional setup/profile label, then start a collection run. Record one stint and mark the pit/control handoff. Stop the car, have a driver ready, then use Stop AI Driver at Rest and verify native AI-OFF readback before taking over. Box, change tires, end the stint, and start the next labeled stint. The run keeps recording through the handoff. End Collection Run closes recording without switching AI off; if AI remains ON, let it drive until safely stopped or exit through the game menu. AI and human samples use the same raw simulator telemetry; controller identity is recorded per sample. Collection has no clock or lap quota. Requested labels are not native readback, and setup/profile identity is unverified without a hash.
